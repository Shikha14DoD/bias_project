# Leadership–Subordination Word Lists (Custom Curation)

## Purpose
Word lists for Measure 3 (Leadership–Subordination Bias) of the gender bias
state-year panel. The project proposal specifies "Garg + custom curation"
for this measure, as no published leadership/subordination list exists in
Caliskan (2017) or Garg (2018).

## Sources anchoring the curation
- Schein, V. E. (1973, 2001) — "think manager–think male" research:
  managerial role descriptors
- Eagly, A. H. & Karau, S. J. (2002) — role congruity theory:
  agentic traits (leadership) vs communal traits (subordination)

## Curation decisions
- "secretary" EXCLUDED from subordination list — overlaps with
  occupations1950.txt (Measure 4) and is itself an occupation
- "intelligent"-type competence words EXCLUDED — reserved for
  Measure 2 (Competence–Warmth) to keep dimensions independent
- Plural forms included (leaders, managers) to improve coverage in
  small corpora
- Lists reviewed for overlap against:
  adjectives_intelligencegeneral.txt, adjectives_sensitive.txt,
  career_terms_weat6.txt, family_terms_weat6.txt

## Known overlaps (documented, retained)
- "executive" appears in career_terms_weat6.txt (Measure 1) — retained
  because leadership and career constructs legitimately share it;
  measures are reported separately
- "ambitious", "confident" are agentic traits per Eagly & Karau —
  retained on leadership side
